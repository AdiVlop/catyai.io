## Quick Start

Add Caty.AI to your website in under 2 minutes:

### 1. Get Your API Key

Sign up at [app.catyai.io](https://app.catyai.io) and create a new widget.

### 2. Add the Widget

```html
<script
  src="https://api.catyai.io/widget.js"
  data-api-key="YOUR_API_KEY">
</script>
```

### 3. Train Your AI

Upload documents, add FAQ entries, or crawl your website to build your knowledge base.

[Read the full guide →](/guide/getting-started)
